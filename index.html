1ConcavityConcavity

The first derivative is the slope of the tangent line; the second derivative describes how that slope changes — the curvature of the graph.

Concave upthe graph lies above its tangent lines
Concave downthe graph lies below its tangent lines
على (−π, 0) الميول تزيد من اليسار لليمين والمنحنى فوق المماسات: تقعر لأعلى. على (0, π) الميول تقل والمنحنى تحت المماسات: تقعر لأسفل.
1ConcavityDefinition 4.2.1 · Theorem 4.2.1
Definition 4.2.1Concave Up or Concave Down
Let $f$ be a differentiable function on an open interval $I$. Then
  1. $f$ is said to be concave up on $I$ if $f'$ is increasing on $I$.
  2. $f$ is said to be concave down on $I$ if $f'$ is decreasing on $I$.
Theorem 4.2.1Concavity Test
Suppose $f''(x)$ exists on an open interval $I$.
  1. If $f''(x) > 0$ on $I$, then $f$ is concave up on $I$.
  2. If $f''(x) < 0$ on $I$, then $f$ is concave down on $I$.
التعريف بدلالة تزايد وتناقص f′، ثم ضغطة تُظهر اختبار التقعر بإشارة f″.

2Inflection pointsDefinition 4.2.2 · Theorem 4.2.2
Definition 4.2.2Inflection Point
Let $f$ be a continuous function on an open interval $I$ and $x_0\in I$. Then the point $(x_0, f(x_0))$ is said to be an inflection point if $f(x)$ changes its concavity (upward to downward or downward to upward) around $x_0$.
Theorem 4.2.2Points of Inflection
Let $(c, f(c))$ be a point of inflection of the graph of the function $f$. Then, either $f''(c) = 0$, or $f''(c)$ does not exist.
$y = x^3$: the concavity changes at $(0,0)$
تعريف نقطة الانقلاب: f متصلة و x₀ داخل الفترة والتقعر يتغير حولها. ثم النظرية: المرشحون هم f″ = 0 أو f″ غير موجودة. ثم مثال x³ عند (0, 0).

3Intervals of concavityProcedure · video
Video not found: upload  concavity-procedure.mp4  to the same folder as this page.
Procedureintervals of concavity & inflection points
  1. Find $D_f$, then $f'$ and $f''$.
  2. Find the points where $f''(x)=0$ or $f''(x)$ does not exist.
  3. Put them on the number line of $f''$ and find its sign on each interval.
  4. $f'' > 0 \Rightarrow$ concave up; $f'' < 0 \Rightarrow$ concave down.
  5. Inflection point at $c$ if the sign changes around $c$ and $c\in D_f$.
الفيديو يشرح الطريقة العامة: خط إشارة f″ ثم أسهم f′ ثم أقواس التقعر لـ f. في الأمثلة التالية نستخدم خط f″ وخط f فقط. الفيديو يحتاج ملف concavity-procedure.mp4 بجانب الصفحة.

4The second derivative testTheorem 4.2.3
Theorem 4.2.3The Second Derivative Test
Suppose $f''$ is continuous on an open interval containing $c$ with $f'(c) = 0$.
  • If $f''(c) > 0$, then $f$ has a relative minimum at $c$.
  • If $f''(c) < 0$, then $f$ has a relative maximum at $c$.
  • If $f''(c) = 0$, then the test is inconclusive; $f$ may have a relative maximum, relative minimum, or neither at $c$.
Remark
In such cases ($f''(c) = 0$), we may use the first derivative test.
اختبار المشتقة الثانية عند النقاط الحرجة f′(c) = 0، وإذا f″(c) = 0 نرجع لاختبار المشتقة الأولى.

5Challenging & CommunicateChallenging Problems 5
Challenging 5
Suppose $f$ and $g$ are both concave up on $(-\infty,\infty)$. Under what condition on $f$ will the composite function $h(x) = f(g(x))$ be concave up?
  1. (Assume $f, g$ twice differentiable.)$h'(x) = f'(g(x))\, g'(x)$
  2. $h''(x) = f''(g(x))\,[g'(x)]^2$$\qquad\quad +\, f'(g(x))\, g''(x)$
  3. $f'' > 0,\ [g']^2 \ge 0$$\Rightarrow$ first term $\ge 0$$g'' > 0$$\Rightarrow h''(x) > 0$whenever $f'(g(x)) > 0$
Sufficient condition$f$ increasing ($f' > 0$) $\Rightarrow h = f\circ g$ is concave up

The condition is sufficient. The next two slides show that without it concavity may fail, and that it is not necessary.

نشتق مرتين بقاعدة السلسلة. الحد الأول غير سالب دائماً، والثاني موجب إذا كانت f تزايدية: شرط كافٍ. الشريحتان التاليتان: قد يفشل بدونه، وليس ضرورياً.

Challenging 5
Suppose $f$ and $g$ are both concave up on $(-\infty,\infty)$. Under what condition on $f$ will the composite function $h(x) = f(g(x))$ be concave up?
  1. Take $f$ concave up but decreasing:$f(u)=e^{-u},\quad g(x)=x^2$
  2. $h(x)=f(g(x))=e^{-x^2}$
  3. $h''(x)=(4x^2-2)\,e^{-x^2}$
  4. $h''(0)=-2<0$
So$h$ is concave down on $\left(-\tfrac{1}{\sqrt2},\tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}\right)$: without the condition, concavity may fail
$h(x)=e^{-x^2}$ — teal: concave up, gold: concave down
f متناقصة: e^(−u)، و g = x². الناتج منحنى الجرس، مقعر لأسفل حول الصفر. الرسم يظهر بعد الحساب.

Challenging 5
Suppose $f$ and $g$ are both concave up on $(-\infty,\infty)$. Under what condition on $f$ will the composite function $h(x) = f(g(x))$ be concave up?
  1. Again $f$ concave up and decreasing:$f(u)=e^{-u}$
  2. but now$g(x)=e^{x}+x,\quad g''(x)=e^{x}>0$
  3. $h(x)=e^{-(e^{x}+x)}$
  4. $h''(x)=e^{-g}\left[(g')^2-g''\right]$$\phantom{h''(x)}=e^{-g}\left(e^{2x}+e^{x}+1\right)>0$
So$h$ is concave up on $\mathbb{R}$ although $f'<0$: the condition is not necessary
Exact requirement$f''(g)\,(g')^2+f'(g)\,g''>0$ — it depends on $g$ too
$h(x)=e^{-(e^x+x)}$ — concave up everywhere
نفس f المتناقصة لكن g مختلفة: الحد الأول يغلب الثاني فتبقى h مقعرة لأعلى. إذن الشرط كافٍ وليس ضرورياً، والشرط الدقيق يعتمد على g أيضاً.

Communicate 2
Prove that a quadratic function $f(x) = ax^2 + bx + c$ has no inflection points.
  1. $f$ is quadratic $\Rightarrow a\ne0$
  2. $f'(x) = 2ax + b$
  3. $f''(x) = 2a \ne 0$ for all $x$
  4. $f''$ exists everywhere and is never $0$: by Theorem 4.2.2 there is no candidate $c$ with $f''(c) = 0$ or $f''(c)$ undefined($a>0$: concave up on $\mathbb{R}$; $a<0$: concave down on $\mathbb{R}$)
Conclusion$\Rightarrow f$ has no inflection points  ■
f″ = 2a ثابتة لا تساوي صفراً، فلا يوجد مرشح لنقطة انقلاب حسب النظرية 4.2.2.
